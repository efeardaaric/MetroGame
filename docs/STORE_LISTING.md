## English

**Name:** Metro Tycoon: Türkiye
**Subtitle (30):** City Transit Simulator
**Short description:** Build your own metro network in Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir.

**Full description:**
You are the city's transit planner. On maps based on the real geography of Istanbul, Ankara and
Izmir, build metro, tram, metrobus, commuter rail, funicular and ferry lines.

• Bore deep tunnels under the Bosphorus, cross the Golden Horn, link both shores of the gulf by ferry.
• Choose shallow or deep tunnels and inspect each line's cross-section.
• Read population and attraction maps to learn where riders want to go.
• Tune vehicles and frequency, expand depots, avoid overcrowding.
• Balance fares, city grants and operating costs; review your annual report.
• Complete city goals, earn stars, unlock new cities.
• No ads, no in-app purchases. Plays offline.

**Age rating:** 3+ / Everyone. **Category:** Strategy / Simulation.
