2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Metro Tycoon: Türkiye is a single-player city transit simulation and strategy game. The player is the transit planner of a Turkish city (Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Eskisehir, Antalya, Bursa, Trabzon) starting in 2010 and builds metro, tram, BRT, commuter rail, funicular, gondola and ferry lines on a stylised map. Value: a relaxing but deep planning game about public transport, budgets and city growth. Audience: players aged 4+ who enjoy simulation, strategy and city-builder games, especially people interested in Turkish cities and public transit. It is a paid-upfront game with no ads and no in-app purchases.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account or sample files are needed. The app runs fully offline.
- Launch the app and tap "Play" (Turkish: "Oyna"). Istanbul and Ankara are unlocked from the start; other cities unlock by earning stars.
- Pick Istanbul. A short tutorial explains the controls (pinch to zoom, drag to pan).
- Tap "Build" in the bottom bar, choose Metro, tap the map to place stops, then tap "Finish" to open the line.
- Use the bottom bar to open Lines (add vehicles), Map layers, Goals, Finance, the in-game social feed and Research. The election countdown and upcoming real-world events are shown under the top bar; tap them for details.
- Time controls are at the top right (pause / play / fast forward).
- The main menu also offers Scenarios, How to Play and Achievements.
The language follows the device language (Turkish for Turkish devices, English otherwise).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app does not use any network service, server, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment processor or AI service. It is built with the open-source Godot Engine (MIT licence), which is compiled into the app. No data is collected.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works identically in all regions where it is available; there are no region-specific features or content. It is offered in Turkish and English. It is not distributed in China mainland or Vietnam.
