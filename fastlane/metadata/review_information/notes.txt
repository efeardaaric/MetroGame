Metro Tycoon: Türkiye is a fully offline single-player city transit simulation. No login, no network access, no in-app purchases, no ads, no data collection. To play: tap Play, pick İstanbul, choose a transit mode in the build bar, tap the map to place stops and tap Finish. Real-world events (summits, festivals) and local elections are referenced by name for historical flavour only; the game is not affiliated with any organisation.
