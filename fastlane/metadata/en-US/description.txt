You are the city's transit planner! Build metro networks on the real geography of Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Eskişehir, Antalya, Bursa and Trabzon, starting in 2010, before the big rail investments began.

RACE AGAINST TIME
• Local elections follow the real calendar: March 2014, 2019, 2024, 2029… Win half the vote or you are out of office.
• Make a campaign promise: new lines, more riders or happier commuters. Keep it, and voters reward you.
• Real events are announced months ahead: a NATO summit and the Republic's centenary in Ankara, G20, EXPO 2016 and COP31 in Antalya, the Istanbul Marathon and a European cup final… Serve the venue in time for a reward, or face traffic chaos.

A LIVING MAP
• Metro, tram, BRT, commuter rail, funicular, gondola and ferries.
• Dig deep tunnels under the Bosphorus, cross the Golden Horn, link both shores of a bay by ferry.
• On derby day roads lock up, bridges jam and crowds stream to the stadium, live on the map.
• Day and night, snow, rain and fog; gulls, ferry horns and tram bells.

THE CITY TALKS ABOUT YOU
• Every decision gets a reaction on the in-game social network; important posts pop up on screen.
• Strikes, earthquakes, breakdowns and tough decisions.

DEPTH
• Research: contactless fares, CBTC signalling, driverless metro…
• Budgets, loans, fares and maintenance policy.
• Scenarios, a weekly challenge, sandbox mode and achievements.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Plays fully offline.
