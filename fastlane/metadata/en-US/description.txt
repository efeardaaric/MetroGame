It's 2010. The city doesn't have a single metro line and the roads lock up every morning. You're the new transit planner.

Draw metro, tram, busway, commuter rail, funicular, gondola and ferry lines on the real maps of Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Eskişehir, Antalya, Bursa and Trabzon. Tunnel under the Bosphorus, link both shores of the Gulf by ferry, run a gondola up Boztepe.

But hurry. Local elections follow the real calendar: March 2014, 2019, 2024. Voters look at how happy riders are, how much of the city your lines reach and whether you kept your promise. Fall under fifty percent and you're out. Meanwhile the city keeps moving: the Republic's centenary, a NATO summit in Ankara, G20, EXPO and COP31 in Antalya, the Istanbul Marathon every November. Get a station to the venue in time and you earn money and goodwill; miss it and you watch the roads turn red on the map.

Every decision gets a reaction. On the in-game social network people ask for more trains, complain about the air conditioning and lose their simit to a gull on the ferry. Derby days jam the roads, snow strands the buses, and the metro keeps running.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet needed.
